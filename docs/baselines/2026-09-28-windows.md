# Baseline: Windows, 2026-09-28

**Machine:** Ryzen AI 9 HX 370, Radeon 890M driving a 3840×2400 60 Hz panel (300% scale), on AC. Measured with `kolada-bench` (see [benchmarking.md](../benchmarking.md)). Each row is 30 runs after 2 warmups, launched via `--shell`.

| App / scenario | window shown p50 | first pixel p50 | **98% complete p50** | 98% complete p95 | 100% complete p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Obsidian 1.x, fully closed (real launch) | 738 ms | 756 ms | **1,623 ms** | 1,708 ms | 4,105 ms |
| Obsidian 1.x, tray-resident (window re-show) | 598 ms | 608 ms | **658 ms** | 703 ms | 658 ms |
| Windows 11 Notepad (15 runs) | 330 ms | 345 ms | **723 ms** | 756 ms | 3,007 ms |

**Notes**
- The Obsidian vault has 1,659 notes and 8 plugins, including Smart Connections and lazy-plugins.
- In the resident case the tray plugin keeps Obsidian running. The ~600 ms is mostly the second `Obsidian.exe` process starting up and handing off to the running one. The ~400 ms felt in daily use is likely this path or a tray-icon click.
- One resident run was excluded: Obsidian hid its own window about 1.4 s after showing it.
- The "100% complete" column is driven by late cosmetic changes:
  - Notepad: its WinUI scrollbar collapses at about 3 s.
  - Obsidian: a small change at about 4.1 s, likely a lazily loaded plugin.

**Kolada's M1 gate:** 98% complete p50 ≤ 50 ms and p95 ≤ 80 ms on this machine. That is about 32× faster than Obsidian's real launch and about 13× faster than its tray re-show.
